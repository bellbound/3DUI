
[center][b][size=6]3DUI[/size]

[/b]
[quote][b]A library for creating 3D floating UIs for Skyrim VR with automatic layout, VR interaction (hover, grab, scroll), and flexible
anchoring. This is a Modder's resource that does nothing on its own.[/b][/quote]
[/center]

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Compatible with the vast majority of mods
Some mods that change all projectiles at the engine level might be incompatible
[i]Spell Bender VR[/i] and [i]Universal Arrow Spin[/i] are compatible

[size=5][b]Beta
[/b][/size]3DUI is still in Beta, until I can get some real user feedback.
That said, even in beta, it is very unlikely to crash or otherwise
negatively impact your game when no 3DUI menus are open.

It is safe to install / uninstall at any point.
